Mini Biography: John Cena

John Felix Anthony Cena an American actor and retired professional wrestler.

Wrestling Career

Cena began professional wrestling in 1999 and signed with the World Wrestling Federation in 2001.

Wrestling Achievement

Cena wrestled for WWE for 24 years, becoming a record-setting 17-time world champion, before transitioning fully into his acting career.

Acting Aareer

Cena began acting in 2006 and has done both comedy and action films, receiving praise for his roles in Trainwreck (2015), Blockers (2018), and The Suicide Squad (2021). He has also starred in films such as The Marine (2006), Bumblebee (2018), and F9 (2021), as well as portraying the title character in the superhero series Peacemaker (2022–2025).
